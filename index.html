PROJECT INFO

ABOUT

A browser-based first-person shooter built with Three.js and Socket.io. Practice your aim solo against targets, or drop into real-time multiplayer with up to 4 players per room. Two game modes are available: Free for All and Search & Destroy.

MOVEMENT

Run with WASD, hold SHIFT to walk quietly, C to crouch, and SPACE to jump. Sprinting drains stamina — stand still or walk to recover it.

COMBAT

Left-click to fire. Press R to reload — you carry 54 reserve rounds (10 per mag), replenished only on respawn. Damage falls off with distance. Headshots deal 25 damage and show a red popup. Body shots deal 15 damage and show a yellow popup.

Press Q to throw your pistol — it bounces off walls and floors with real physics. If it hits an enemy it deals 35 damage and flies back to you after 2 seconds. Miss and it returns automatically after 4–5 seconds. You cannot fire while it is in flight.

Press G to throw a grenade (2 per life, recharge over time). Grenades bounce off walls and deal up to 60 damage within their blast radius. Watch out — your own explosions hurt.

String together kills for streak announcements: Triple Kill (3), Rampage (5), Unstoppable (6+).

SEARCH & DESTROY

A 5-round competitive mode. Attackers must plant the C4 bomb at the designated site; defenders must stop them. One team scores a point by eliminating all enemies, planting and detonating the bomb, or defusing it before it explodes.

The bomb carrier is randomly assigned each round. Walk into the bomb site and hold E for 7 seconds to plant (7s). Defenders hold E near the planted bomb for 12 seconds to defuse. The bomb fuse is 50 seconds — you will hear it tick faster as time runs out.

Teams swap sides after round 2. Scores follow the players, not the roles. First to 3 points or the team ahead after 5 rounds wins. On death you enter free-look spectate — use WASD to fly around.

The host starts the match from the lobby and can call a Rematch or Quit after the final round.

MULTIPLAYER

Click 🌐 to open the lobby. Create a named room (choose Free for All or Search & Destroy), share the invite link, paste a link or room code to join a friend, or browse Public Rooms for open sessions. Up to 4 players per room. Hold TAB for the live leaderboard and press ENTER to open chat.

TOOLBAR

Top-right buttons (left to right): Controls guide, Project info, Reset score, Day/Night toggle, Audio settings, Multiplayer lobby.

NOTE

Open spaces inside walls (e.g. windows) currently act as solid barriers — you cannot jump through them or throw objects through them. This will be addressed in a future update.
