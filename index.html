FORGE

ABOUT

A browser-based first-person shooter built with Three.js and Socket.io. Practice your aim solo against targets, or drop into real-time multiplayer with up to 4 players per room. Two game modes are available: Free for All and Search & Destroy.

Built from scratch — no game engine, no shortcuts.

MOVEMENT

Run with WASD, hold SHIFT to walk quietly, C to crouch, and SPACE to jump. Sprinting drains stamina — stand still or walk to recover it. When stamina runs critically low a warning appears on screen; if you keep sprinting on empty stamina you will begin losing health.

WEAPONS

Press 1 to equip the Shotgun (SPAS-12) and 2 to equip the Pistol. Switching plays a smooth holster and draw animation.

Pistol — Semi-automatic, fires a single 9mm round. Headshots deal 25 damage, body shots deal 15 damage. Carries 10 rounds per magazine with 54 in reserve. Damage falls off gradually over distance.

Shotgun — Fires 8 pellets per shell. Headshot pellets deal 45 damage, body shot pellets deal 20 damage each. Devastating at close range but pellets spread and lose energy quickly over distance. Holds 8 shells, reloaded one at a time — press R to reload. Switching weapons cancels an active reload.

COMBAT

Left-click to fire. Press R to reload. Ammo is replenished only on respawn.

Press Q to throw your pistol as a melee weapon — it travels with real physics, bouncing off walls, floors, and players. Hitting an enemy deals 35 damage and the pistol ricochets off them before flying back to your hand. You cannot fire while the pistol is in flight.

Press G to throw a grenade (2 per life, recharge over time). Grenades bounce off walls and deal up to 60 damage within their blast radius. Your own explosions can hurt you.

Explosive Barrels — Red barrels placed around the map can be detonated: 4 pistol shots, 1 shotgun blast, or a grenade blast sets one off. The explosion deals 70 damage within 10 units and falls off to zero at 20 units — you are not safe. Barrels close together trigger a chain reaction, detonating one after another with a 1-second stagger. After exploding, a barrel is disabled for 30 seconds (greyed out with a countdown timer) before restoring to full strength.

Press F to toggle your flashlight. String together kills for streak announcements: Triple Kill (3), Rampage (5), Unstoppable (6+).

SEARCH & DESTROY

A 5-round competitive mode. Attackers must plant the C4 bomb at the designated site; defenders must stop them. One team scores a point by eliminating all enemies, planting and detonating the bomb, or defusing it before it explodes.

The bomb carrier is randomly assigned each round. Walk into the bomb site — a ghost C4 preview appears at your crosshair showing the exact plant location. Hold E for 7 seconds to plant; you crouch automatically during the sequence. Defenders hold E near the planted bomb for 12 seconds to defuse. The bomb fuse is 50 seconds — you will hear it tick faster as time runs out.

Teams swap sides after round 2. Scores follow the players, not the roles. First to 3 points or the team ahead after 5 rounds wins. On death you enter free-look spectate — use WASD to fly around.

The host starts the match from the lobby and can call a Rematch or Quit after the final round.

MULTIPLAYER

Click the Multiplayer icon to open the lobby. Create a named room (choose Free for All or Search & Destroy), share the invite link, paste a link or room code to join a friend, or browse Public Rooms for open sessions. Up to 4 players per room. Hold TAB for the live leaderboard and press ENTER to open chat.

TOOLBAR

Top-right buttons (left to right): Controls Guide, Project Info, Reset Score, Day/Night Toggle, Audio Settings, Multiplayer Lobby.

NOTE

Open spaces inside walls (e.g. windows) currently act as solid barriers — you cannot jump through them or throw objects through them. This will be addressed in a future update.
